Time Series Forecasting

This project involved developing a time series forecasting module designed to predict key business metrics while ensuring consistency across hierarchical levels through reconciliation. The module utilized advanced forecasting techniques such as ARIMA, Prophet, and Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks to generate accurate predictions. Reconciliation algorithms were implemented to align forecasts across multiple aggregation levels, ensuring that detailed-level predictions rolled up correctly to higher-level summaries. This solution provided actionable insights for demand planning, inventory optimization, and financial forecasting, enabling data-driven decision-making and improved operational efficiency.
